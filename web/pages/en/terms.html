The service

bitg converts Uzbek text between the old Latin alphabet, Cyrillic and the new Uzbek Latin alphabet adopted in 2026. It is offered free of charge, with no account, subscription, advertising or paid features.

License

The source code is available under the MIT or Apache 2.0 license, at your option. Dictionaries and fonts keep their own licenses, listed in the repository.

Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to disrupt the website, the Telegram bot or the MCP server, bypass rate limits, or use them to process unlawful content.
  • The MCP server accepts up to 20,000 characters per request and up to 120 requests per minute from one address. For bulk automated use, run the open-source engine yourself.
  • Traffic that harms the service for other users may be limited or blocked.

Accuracy

Conversion is rule-based and checked against dictionaries, but no converter is perfect. Review important documents (legal, official, financial) before you publish or sign them. You are responsible for the text you produce with bitg.

bitg is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, and the authors are not liable for damages arising from its use, as set out in the licenses above. The online services may change or be unavailable at times.

Privacy

What each part of bitg can see is described in the privacy policy. In short, the text you convert is not stored.
